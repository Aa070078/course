FOR MENTORS · BEFORE YOU FACILITATE

Know what to unlock,
not what to lecture.

Use this guide to prepare each project. Mentors explain the engineering context, ask the right questions, and unblock teams. Students learn by shipping the project—not by watching a long lecture.

30 project sessions4 parallel responsibilities1 shared Definition of Done
01

Before

Read the project brief, verify the prerequisites, prepare a small failing example, and confirm the team has the previous handoff.

02

During

Open with the product problem, run a short decision workshop, then coach through questions, reviews, and integration—not step-by-step typing.

03

After

Check the Definition of Done, capture risks and decisions, and nominate the next project only when the current handoff is usable.
