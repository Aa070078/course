THE CURRICULUM

Each module has a shared studio, focused craft instruction, and one integrated artifact. No track can finish alone.

PROJECT PROGRESSION

Not clones.
Products with consequences.

01 · WEEKS 5–8

Service access
feature

Improve one high-friction journey for a campus or civic service using interviews, an accessible interface, and a thin API.

  • 5 user conversations
  • 1 vertical slice
  • 1 observed usability test
02 · WEEKS 11–15

Community operations
increment

Add scheduling and staff workflows to a live shared codebase. Mid-sprint policy changes force contract negotiation and migration.

  • Role-based workflows
  • Design system contribution
  • Staged release + retro
03 · SEMESTER 02

Partner product
capstone

Discover, scope, build, secure, deploy, and operate a useful product for a real partner—then hand it to the next team.

  • 3 public increments
  • Measured outcomes
  • Runbook + handover

RESEARCH FOUNDATIONS

Built for the team
students will join.

The program prioritizes durable practices over tool collecting: accessibility as a requirement, short feedback loops, secure defaults, documented decisions, and responsible human review of AI-assisted work.

01W3C · WCAG 2.2

Accessibility criteria shape design, implementation, and verification—not a final audit.

↗ 02GitHub · Collaboration

Issues, branches, pull requests, review, and protected workflows form the studio’s operating system.

↗ 03DORA · Delivery research

Small batches, user focus, quality feedback, and healthy teams guide project cadence.

↗ 04OWASP · Top 10

Threat modeling, access control, validation, dependencies, and logging enter before deployment.

↗ 05Stack Overflow · 2025 survey

Technology choices and AI literacy reflect current practice while fundamentals stay central.

↗ 06NIST · AI RMF

Students disclose, verify, test, and document AI assistance instead of outsourcing judgment.

↗

Curriculum reviewed annually against current job postings, platform guidance, accessibility standards, graduate interviews, and partner feedback.
